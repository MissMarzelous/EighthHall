# Changelog

## 1.0.0 - 2026-10-23

First release.

- 8-line feedback delay network reverb engine with modulated, Hermite-interpolated delay lines, in-loop damping and exact RT60 control
- Controls: Size, Decay, Pre-Delay, Diffusion, Damping, Low Cut, High Cut, Mod Rate, Mod Depth, Width, Mix, Freeze
- 9 factory presets
- Resizable custom GUI with live decay graph and output meters
- VST3 (Windows/macOS/Linux), AU (macOS), LV2 (Linux) and Standalone builds
- Automated DSP test bench; pluginval strictness 10 and auval in CI
